What we can say plainly
4.2 reduced the response wait from 90 to 60 seconds and increased proximity’s ranking weight. Misses increased, offers shifted sharply between responders, and overall acceptance concealed some of that harm.
The supplied code contains a plausible amplifier: a miss lowers history, history affects order, and only accepting earns points back. We have not established every historical ranking or the actual time each person saw an offer.
Prototype ready for discussion · deployment not established
Before a production pilot
  1. 01Name the accountable owner and agree prototype and pilot success criteria.
  2. 02Validate candidate eligibility, score recovery and the 75-second proposal with engineering and data.
  3. 03Measure send/display/expiry timing. Protect assignment speed, required skills and Supply’s availability contract.
  4. 04Test with handlers and responders, run the checklist again, then stage experiments so effects can be separated.
